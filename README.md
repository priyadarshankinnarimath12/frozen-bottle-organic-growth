# Frozen Bottle - SEO Growth Case Study

Scaled Frozen Bottle’s monthly organic traffic from 32,039 to 147,452 in 12 months (+360%). Achieved 4.6× traffic growth, +195% branded search growth, 500+ programmatic pages, 10-12 daily organic leads from other cities, and 3-4× search visibility growth.

## Frozen Bottle

### Objective

Scale organic traffic while expanding branded search, keyword visibility, and organic lead generation through a structured, demand-driven SEO strategy.

### Strategy

**Campaign SEO · Product SEO · Programmatic SEO · Content Strategy · Search Intent**

### Execution

- Published **500+ programmatic articles and city-focused pages**
- Published **2 new blogs and refreshed 1 existing article every week**
- Built SEO opportunities around campaigns, offers, and product launches
- Targeted **transactional, navigational, and local** search intent
- Used campaign and search demand signals to identify content opportunities
- Expanded visibility across **AI Overviews, People Also Ask, featured snippets, and branded search**

### Results

- **32K → 147K** monthly organic traffic
- **4.6×** organic traffic growth
- **+195%** branded search growth
- **500+** programmatic articles and city-focused pages
- **10-12** organic leads per day from other cities
- **3-4×** search visibility growth

## How I Approach SEO

**Understand the search → Build for the intent → Optimise → Measure → Iterate**

I look beyond rankings, focusing on how **campaign demand, search intent, and scalable content translate into organic traffic, visibility, and business outcomes.**
